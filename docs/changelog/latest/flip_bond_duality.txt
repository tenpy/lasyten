- New functions :func:`lasyten.tensors.flip_bond_duality` (C++ ``lasyten::flip_bond_duality``) and
  :func:`lasyten.tensors.flip_leg_duality` (C++ ``lasyten::flip_leg_duality``).
  ``flip_bond_duality`` flips the duality (``is_dual``) of a bond between two contracted legs of two tensors, as ``Z``
  on one end and ``Z^{-1}`` on the other. It is a gauge transformation of the bond: the contraction is unchanged
  (similar to :func:`lasyten.tensors.move_hidden_leg`). ``flip_leg_duality`` flips a single leg and thereby *changes*
  the tensor by ``Z`` (not on legs with only the trivial sector, e.g. the dummy legs of a finite MPO); keeping the
  network consistent is up to the caller. Together, they are the missing ingredient for ``dagger`` on a chain of tensors
  (e.g. an MPO), which dualizes all virtual legs.
- New function :func:`lasyten.models.couplings.dagger_factorization`: hermitian conjugate of an MPO-like
  chain of ``[wL, p, wR, p*]`` tensors, linear in the number of tensors. ``Coupling.dagger`` now uses it
  instead of contracting the full operator, and keeps the virtual leg dualities of the original coupling.
  The parameter ``flip_boundary_legs`` (default ``True``) controls whether the two outer virtual legs are flipped as well,
  which is what is needed for both finite and infinite chains unless the environments of a finite chain are daggered, too.
  Addresses https://github.com/tenpy/lasyten/issues/311.
  Note that the factorization of ``Coupling.dagger()`` now differs from the previous one (which refactorized the
  full operator) by a gauge on the virtual legs, e.g. signs on the odd sector of fermion parity. The operator
  ``Coupling.dagger().to_tensor()`` is unchanged.
- ``flip_bond_duality`` with both ends of the bond on one tensor (``flip_bond_duality(A, A, leg_a, leg_b)``) flips both
  legs of that tensor and returns it twice.
